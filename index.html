Skip rocks. Dodge milk. Down the cows.

How to play
  • Tap rocks on the beach to pocket them. Flat, palm-sized, mid-weight rocks skip best.
  • Flick from the rock in your hand, inside the ring, to throw. A quick clean flick is ideal. Its direction aims.
  • Swipe sideways outside the ring to step left or right. You stay put until you step again.
  • Cows glow before they spray. Step out of the way or your lactose intolerance climbs. 100% ends the game.
