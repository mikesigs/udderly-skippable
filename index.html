Lakeside Cow Combat

UDDERLY SKIPPABLE

Skip flat rocks at the flying cows before their milk pushes your lactose intolerance to 100%.

  • Tap rocks on the beach to pocket them. Flat, palm-sized, mid-weight rocks skip best.
  • Flick from the rock in your hand to throw, in any direction inside the dotted ring. A quick, clean flick is ideal. Too soft plops, too hard plunges. The direction of your flick aims.
  • Swipe sideways outside the ring to step left or right. You stay there until you step again.
  • Cows fly low over the water and shoot milk. Step out of the way before it lands.
  • Cows get tougher and faster each wave. Hit 100% lactose and it's over.

Practice is the same beach with no cows, for working on your skips. Tap PAUSE in the corner during a game to pause or quit.

Best played holding your phone upright in portrait.
